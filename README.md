## About the course

Employee wellbeing is shaped by the whole system of work — leadership, job design, access to support, trust and personal resources — not by a collection of perks. This course takes HR, wellness and people leaders through that system: separating health from wellness, applying Singapore's privacy and employment requirements to wellbeing data, assessing the needs of different employee groups, choosing technology on evidence rather than fashion, turning policy into day-to-day practice, and communicating and evaluating a programme so that employees can see what changed.

Ten real-use-case activities, each anchored in a published source and separated from fictional training assumptions, connect the concepts to workplace decisions.

## Learning outcomes

- **LO1** — Identify workplace health and wellness trends, technologies and research implications for employees and the organisation.
- **LO2** — Implement inclusive health and wellness programmes for different employee groups and translate policies into day-to-day practices.
- **LO3** — Communicate programme benefits through appropriate channels and assess effectiveness using employee feedback and programme evidence.

## Topics covered

1. **Workplace Health, Wellness and Evidence** — wellbeing dimensions, Singapore workplace requirements and privacy-by-design, factors that shape employee health, needs assessment, trends, technology options and the evidence-to-decision pipeline.
2. **Programme Design and Implementation** — programme portfolios, design criteria, policy-to-practice and manager playbooks, individual and organisational resilience, participation barriers, partner governance, budget and pilot-to-scale gates.
3. **Communication, Feedback and Improvement** — benefits, audience and message design, channels, employee feedback and listening systems, logic models, balanced measures, evaluation threats and management review.

## Use boundary

The real-world anchors are published research, regulatory guidance and public programmes. All named employers, employee data, budgets and operational circumstances in the activities are fictional training assumptions unless a source note says otherwise. Do not use identifiable employee health data in class.

This repository publishes the learner-facing courseware and activities only. Credentials and internal reference material are not included.
