# Facilitator Questionnaire — Activity 3

## Evidence and Technology Selection Board

1. Which source claims are credible, relevant and transferable to this workforce?
2. What employee need does each option address—and what new risk does it create?
3. Which criteria and weights should govern the choice?
4. What pilot result would justify scale, adaptation or termination?

## Challenge questions

1. Which claim is a verified fact, which is an assumption, and which still needs evidence?
2. What would cause your recommendation or recovery priority to change?
3. What dependency or stakeholder could invalidate the plan?
4. How will you demonstrate effective closure rather than completed paperwork?
