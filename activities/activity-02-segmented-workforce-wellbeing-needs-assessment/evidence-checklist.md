# Evidence Checklist — Activity 2

## Segmented Workforce Wellbeing Needs Assessment

- [ ] Public facts are cited and separated from training assumptions.
- [ ] Every scenario question is answered with evidence or a stated limitation.
- [ ] The detailed procedure was followed and each checkpoint is recorded.
- [ ] Owners, decision rights, dates and review triggers are explicit where applicable.
- [ ] Acceptance test met: Every priority uses at least two evidence signals, small groups are suppressed, uncertainty is explicit, no condition is diagnosed, and recommended action addresses a workplace factor plus an access need.
- [ ] The final recommendation states residual risk and the next validation action.
