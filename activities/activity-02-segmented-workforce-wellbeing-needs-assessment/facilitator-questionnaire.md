# Facilitator Questionnaire — Activity 2

## Segmented Workforce Wellbeing Needs Assessment

1. What can each evidence source support, and what can it not support?
2. Which needs are shared and which differ by workforce group?
3. Where could response bias, small numbers or stereotyping distort the result?
4. Which two priorities should management address first, and why?

## Challenge questions

1. Which claim is a verified fact, which is an assumption, and which still needs evidence?
2. What would cause your recommendation or recovery priority to change?
3. What dependency or stakeholder could invalidate the plan?
4. How will you demonstrate effective closure rather than completed paperwork?
