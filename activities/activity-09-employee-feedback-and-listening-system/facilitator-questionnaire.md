# Facilitator Questionnaire — Activity 9

## Employee Feedback and Listening System

1. Which question requires survey, focus group, interview or service data?
2. What data should not be collected, and what reporting threshold is safe?
3. How will employees understand anonymity, confidentiality and escalation?
4. How will management acknowledge, decide and close the feedback loop?

## Challenge questions

1. Which claim is a verified fact, which is an assumption, and which still needs evidence?
2. What would cause your recommendation or recovery priority to change?
3. What dependency or stakeholder could invalidate the plan?
4. How will you demonstrate effective closure rather than completed paperwork?
