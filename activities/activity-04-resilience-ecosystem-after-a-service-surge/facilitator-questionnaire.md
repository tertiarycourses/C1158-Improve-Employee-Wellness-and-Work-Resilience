# Facilitator Questionnaire — Activity 4

## Resilience Ecosystem After a Service Surge

1. Which demands are preventable, reducible or unavoidable?
2. Which resources are missing at individual, team and organisational levels?
3. What sequence balances immediate relief with sustainable redesign?
4. How will leaders know whether the change improves recovery and service?

## Challenge questions

1. Which claim is a verified fact, which is an assumption, and which still needs evidence?
2. What would cause your recommendation or recovery priority to change?
3. What dependency or stakeholder could invalidate the plan?
4. How will you demonstrate effective closure rather than completed paperwork?
