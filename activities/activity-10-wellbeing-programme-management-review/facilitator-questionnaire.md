# Facilitator Questionnaire — Activity 10

## Wellbeing Programme Management Review

1. Did delivery reach the intended groups safely and equitably?
2. What evidence supports changes in experience, work conditions and outcomes?
3. Which alternative explanations and data-quality weaknesses limit confidence?
4. What should management scale, adapt, pause or stop, and what is the next review trigger?

## Challenge questions

1. Which claim is a verified fact, which is an assumption, and which still needs evidence?
2. What would cause your recommendation or recovery priority to change?
3. What dependency or stakeholder could invalidate the plan?
4. How will you demonstrate effective closure rather than completed paperwork?
